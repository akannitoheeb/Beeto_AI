What is Beeto?

Beeto is an AI chatbot for email marketing. You describe the email you need, and it writes subject lines, campaigns and multi-email sequences, then checks them for deliverability and compliance issues.

Do I need email marketing experience?

No. Describe your audience and goal in plain words. Beeto asks follow-up questions when it needs more detail and explains any problem it finds.

What does the deliverability check look at?

It flags spam-prone wording and formatting, and checks for legal requirements under CAN-SPAM, GDPR and CASL, such as unsubscribe links and sender details. It reduces risk, but no tool can promise inbox placement.

Can I use it with my email platform?

Yes. Beeto works in a chat window, so you copy the finished email into whichever platform you already use.

Is Beeto really free?

Yes. The free plan covers writing and checking. Pro costs ₦8,000 or $9 per month if you need more.
