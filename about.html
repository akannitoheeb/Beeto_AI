Beeto — the AI Chatbot Built for Email Marketing

Beeto is a free AI chatbot that writes subject lines, full email campaigns, multi-email sequences, and checks your copy for deliverability issues before you send. Built by Toheeb Akanni, a freelance email marketing strategist and WordPress web designer based in Lagos, Nigeria.

What Beeto does

  • Campaign generation — subject line options, preheader, body, and a clear call-to-action, structured and ready to send.
  • Email sequences — 3 to 5-email drip flows with a realistic send-time cadence, each email built around its own purpose in the sequence.
  • Deliverability checks — flags spam-trigger phrases, missing unsubscribe language, and region-specific compliance gaps (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, CASL).
  • Brand-matched copy — set a brand voice, audience, and words to avoid once, and every generation stays consistent with it.
  • Repurposing — turn one campaign into an SMS message and social captions for Instagram, LinkedIn, and X.
