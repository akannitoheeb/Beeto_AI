Beeto: the AI chatbot built for email marketing

Beeto is a free AI chatbot that writes subject lines, full email campaigns and multi-email sequences, and checks your copy for deliverability issues before you send. Built by Toheeb Akanni, a freelance email marketing strategist and WordPress web designer in Lagos, Nigeria.

  • Campaigns: subject lines, preheader, body and call-to-action, ready to send.
  • Sequences: 3 to 5-email drip flows, each with its own purpose.
  • Deliverability checks: spam triggers and CAN-SPAM, GDPR and CASL gaps.
  • Brand-matched copy: set your voice and audience once.
◐ Private chat: this conversation won't be saved, and won't use memory or your brand profile.
